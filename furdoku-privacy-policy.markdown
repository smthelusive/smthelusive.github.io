*Last updated: September 25, 2026*

Furdoku is a puzzle game for iOS. This page describes what data the app
collects and how it's used.

### What Furdoku collects

Furdoku does not collect, transmit, or have access to any personal data.
There are no user accounts, no network requests, and no analytics or
advertising SDKs of any kind.

The only information the app stores is your own puzzle progress — which
puzzles you've solved and your best times. This is saved locally on your
device using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` storage, and it never leaves
your device. Nobody but you, not even the developer, has access to it.

### In-app purchases

Furdoku may offer optional in-app purchases to unlock additional puzzle
packs. These purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit —
Furdoku does not collect, store, or have access to any payment or purchase
information. Whether a pack is unlocked is checked directly against Apple's
own purchase records on your device, not against anything Furdoku stores
itself.

### Third parties

Furdoku shares no data with any third party. The only exception is Apple,
which processes any in-app purchases directly — see above.

### Data retention and deletion

Your puzzle progress stays on your device for as long as the app is
installed. Deleting the app deletes this data along with it — there's
nothing stored anywhere else to separately request deletion of. Any
purchase records are retained by Apple as part of your Apple ID purchase
history, governed by Apple's own privacy policy, not this one.

### Changes to this policy

If Furdoku's data practices ever change beyond what's described here, this
page will be updated to reflect that before the change ships.
